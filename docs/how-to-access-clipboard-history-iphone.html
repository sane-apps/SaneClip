How to Access Mac Clipboard History on iPhone

You copied a link on your Mac this morning. Now you're on your iPhone and need it. Apple's Universal Clipboard only keeps your last copy, and only for a short time. It's gone. And there's no clipboard history on iPhone at all.

Option 1: The Hard Way (Workarounds)

  • 1
    Email or AirDrop links to yourself every time you copy something important.
  • 2
    Use a Notes doc as a manual clipboard sync between devices.
  • 3
    Pay a yearly subscription for a clipboard app with its own sync.

Option 2: The Sane Way (SaneClip)

  • ✓
    Free iOS App: The SaneClip companion for iPhone and iPad is completely free. Turn on sync in SaneClip Settings > Sync on your Mac, sign in to the same iCloud account on your iPhone, then open the app to refresh.
  • ✓
    Companion History Access: Browse recent synced clips, view pinned items, search your history, and see source-aware colors. Tap any item to copy it on iPhone.
  • ✓
    Current iPhone Clipboard Save: iOS exposes the current pasteboard, not old overwritten copies. Use the + button or Share sheet to save what is available now.

Questions

Does iPhone have a clipboard history?

No. iOS keeps only the current item. Universal Clipboard can paste the last thing you copied on your Mac for a short time, but it keeps no history.

Is the SaneClip iPhone app free?

Yes. The iPhone and iPad companion is a free App Store download. On the Mac, SaneClip is $14.99 once after a 14-day trial.

Do my clips go through SaneApps servers?

No. Sync runs through your own iCloud account, and SaneApps never receives your clipboard contents.
