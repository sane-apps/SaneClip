Text Snippets and Templates on Mac

You type the same email sign-off, meeting template, or code block a dozen times a day. Here are three ways to save reusable text snippets on your Mac, from free and limited to powerful and affordable.

Option 1: macOS Text Replacements

  • 1
    Open System Settings > Keyboard > Text Replacements.
  • 2
    Add a shortcut like "@@sig" that expands to your email signature.
  • 3
    Limitations: plain text only, no placeholders, no dynamic dates, and it does not work in every app.

Option 2: TextExpander (subscription)

  • 1
    Full-featured snippet manager with fill-in fields and scripting.
  • 2
    Pro costs $60 a year, billed annually, and there is a free plan with fewer features (TextExpander pricing, checked 12 Nov 2026).
  • 3
    You pay every year for as long as you use it.

Option 3: SaneClip Snippets ($14.99 once)

  • ✓
    Dynamic placeholders: Use {{name}} to prompt for input, {{date}} and {{time}} for auto-fill, and {{clipboard}} to insert the current clipboard.
  • ✓
    Categories and search: Group snippets into categories and find them by name or content.
  • ✓
    Siri Shortcuts: Paste any snippet by name from Shortcuts.app or a voice command.
  • ✓
    URL scheme: Trigger snippets programmatically with saneclip://snippet?name=MySnippet.
  • ✓
    100% local, one-time purchase. Your templates never leave your Mac. No subscription, no cloud lock-in.

Questions

What placeholders can a SaneClip snippet use?

{{date}} and {{time}} fill in the current date and time, {{clipboard}} inserts what you last copied, and any other name in double braces, like {{name}}, asks you for a value when you paste.

Are snippets a Pro feature?

Yes. SaneClip Pro is $14.99 once, after a free 14-day trial with no card.

How is this different from macOS Text Replacements?

Text Replacements turn a typed shortcut into fixed text. SaneClip snippets add placeholders, categories, search, and pasting by name from Shortcuts or a URL.
