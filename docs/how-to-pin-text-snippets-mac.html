Questions

Do pinned clips ever get deleted?

No. Auto-delete skips pinned clips, and SaneClip keeps them even when new copies push older ones out of history.

Is pinning a Pro feature?

No. Pinning is free. Pro adds snippets with placeholders, tags, collections and notes.

Can I paste a pinned clip with a shortcut?

Open history with Control-Shift-Command-Y, select the clip and press Return. Control-Command-1 to 9 paste your nine most recent clips, not pinned ones.
