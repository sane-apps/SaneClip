How to Rewrite and Summarize Copied Text on Mac

You copied a long email, a meeting note or a messy paragraph, and you want a shorter or cleaner version. SaneClip can rewrite it, summarize it or pull out the key points. It uses Apple's language model on your Mac, so the text is not sent anywhere.

Quick answer
  • Open your SaneClip history and Control-click a text clip.
  • Choose AI — On Device (macOS 26+), then Rewrite, Summarize or Extract Key Points.
  • Read the result, then click Copy or Cancel.

What you need

  • macOS 26 or later. SaneClip itself runs on macOS 14, but the AI actions need macOS 26.
  • A Mac that supports Apple Intelligence. Apple lists Macs with M1 or later.
  • Apple Intelligence turned on in System Settings, and Apple's model downloaded and ready.

Apple Intelligence is not offered in every language or region, so check Apple's page if the setting is missing.

Steps

  • 1
    Copy the text as usual. SaneClip saves it in your history.
  • 2
    Open your history: click the SaneClip icon in the menu bar, or press Command-Shift-Control-Y (the default shortcut).
  • 3
    Control-click (or right-click) the text clip.
  • 4
    Choose AI — On Device (macOS 26+), then pick an action.
  • 5
    A preview opens and shows Working entirely on this Mac… while the model runs. When the result appears, click Copy to put it on your clipboard, or Cancel to close it.

What each action does

  • Rewrite makes the text clearer and shorter, and keeps its meaning and tone.
  • Summarize boils the text down to one short paragraph.
  • Extract Key Points turns it into a short bullet list of the main points.

Your original clip stays as it was. The result is not added to your history, so you choose where it goes.

What it can't do

  • Long text: a clip can be about 2,000 characters of plain English, roughly 300 words. Longer clips show “This clip is too long for on-device AI.” Copy a shorter part and try again.
  • Images: the menu only appears on text clips.
  • Older Macs: on macOS 14 or 15 the menu still appears, but picking an action shows On-Device AI Unavailable with “On-device AI requires macOS 26 or later.” If Apple Intelligence is off, it asks you to turn it on in System Settings. The rest of SaneClip works as usual.
  • Perfect results: it is a small model that runs on your Mac. Read the result before you paste it somewhere that matters.

Questions

Is my text sent to a server?

No. SaneClip uses Apple's Foundation Models framework, which runs the language model on your Mac. SaneClip does not send the clip to an online AI service.

Why does it say On-Device AI Unavailable?

Your Mac is missing something the model needs: macOS 26 or later, a Mac that supports Apple Intelligence, Apple Intelligence turned on, or the model finished downloading. The message tells you which one.

Is this a Pro feature?

The AI menu has no extra lock or charge. It works during the 14-day trial with every Pro feature, and after that with SaneClip Pro at $14.99 once.
