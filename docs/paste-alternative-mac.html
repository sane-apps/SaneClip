Paste Alternative for Mac: Paste vs SaneClip

Paste is one of the best-known clipboard managers on the Mac, and an Apple Editors' Choice. If you're looking for an alternative, it's usually for one of three reasons: you'd rather pay once, you want your history locked and encrypted, or you only need it on a Mac. Here's an honest side-by-side. Every Paste fact comes from Paste's own App Store listing or pasteapp.io, checked on 17 Oct 2026.

Short answer
  • Stay with Paste if you share pinboards with a team, want an iOS keyboard, or connect your clipboard to AI tools over MCP.
  • Try SaneClip if you want a one-time $14.99 price, Touch ID lock and on-disk encryption, and password-manager copies kept out automatically.
  • Want free? Maccy (open source) or, on macOS 26 Tahoe and later, Spotlight's built-in clipboard history. See all clipboard managers compared.

Where Paste is the better pick

Paste is built for people who work across Mac, iPhone and iPad and with other people. Pinboards can be shared with view or edit rights, the iOS keyboard brings your history into any app, and on the Mac it can hand your clipboard history to AI tools like Claude and Cursor over MCP. It can also hide itself from screen sharing. If those matter to you, Paste is worth its price.

Questions people ask

Is there a Paste alternative without a subscription?

Yes. SaneClip is $14.99 once after a 14-day trial with no card. Paste itself also sells a one-time Lifetime plan ($99.99 in its US App Store listing) alongside its $29.99 annual and $3.99 monthly plans. Maccy is free and open source but doesn't sync to iPhone.

Does SaneClip sync with iPhone like Paste?

Yes. SaneClip has a free iPhone and iPad companion app that syncs through your own iCloud account. Paste also syncs Mac, iPhone and iPad through your private iCloud and adds a Paste Keyboard on iOS.

Can SaneClip lock clipboard history with Touch ID?

Yes. SaneClip Pro locks history behind Touch ID or your Mac password and encrypts it at rest with AES-256-GCM. Paste's listing doesn't mention a history lock or on-disk encryption.

What does Paste do that SaneClip doesn't?

Paste lets you share pinboards with your team, family or clients, has built-in MCP support for AI tools on Mac, can hide itself from screen sharing, and has an iOS keyboard. SaneClip doesn't list those features.

Does SaneClip run on Intel Macs?

No. SaneClip for Mac needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later on an Apple silicon Mac. Paste's Mac app requires macOS 14 or later.
