Pastebot Alternative for Mac: Pastebot 3 vs SaneClip

Pastebot 3 from Tapbots is a much-loved power-user clipboard manager. It also requires macOS 26 Tahoe, so if you're on Sonoma or Sequoia, or you want your history locked and encrypted, you may be looking around. Here's an honest comparison. Every Pastebot fact comes from Tapbots' own Pastebot page, checked on 17 Oct 2026.

Short answer
  • Stay with Pastebot if you're on Tahoe and you live in custom filters, Smart Pastebins or its command-line tool.
  • Try SaneClip if you're on macOS 14 or 15, or you want Touch ID lock, on-disk encryption, OCR and an iPhone companion for $14.99 once.

Where Pastebot is the better pick

Pastebot's filters are its superpower: stack base filters into your own, give them shortcuts and watch them work live. Smart Pastebins sort clippings by rules, every clipping can get its own shortcut, and there's a command-line tool for scripting. You own the version you paid for, and Tapbots offers a 30-day refund when you buy direct. If you're on Tahoe and that's how you work, Pastebot is a great choice.

Questions people ask

Does Pastebot 3 work on macOS Sonoma or Sequoia?

Tapbots says Pastebot 3 requires macOS 26 Tahoe or later, and offers the older Pastebot 2 as a separate download. SaneClip runs on macOS 14 Sonoma or later on Apple silicon.

How much does Pastebot cost compared with SaneClip?

Bought direct, Pastebot 3 is $39 with one year of updates, and another year of updates is $19. You keep the last version you paid for. SaneClip is $14.99 once. Both have a 14-day free trial.

Does SaneClip have Pastebot-style stacks?

Yes. SaneClip's paste stack lets you queue clips and paste them in order, first-in-first-out or last-in-first-out. Pastebot pastes its stack in sequence with Control-Command-V.

Can SaneClip build custom filters like Pastebot?

Not in the same way. Pastebot lets you build complex filters by stacking base filters and preview them live. SaneClip has a fixed set of text transforms, such as UPPERCASE, Title Case, JSON formatting and stripping HTML or Markdown.

Can SaneClip lock clipboard history with Touch ID?

Yes. SaneClip Pro locks history behind Touch ID or your Mac password and encrypts it at rest with AES-256-GCM. Pastebot's page doesn't mention a history lock or encryption.
